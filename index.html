Biography

I am currently pursuing a PhD degree at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (AMSS) and University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), supervised by Prof. Xiaohong Jia. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from UCAS in 2021. My research interest includes computer graphics, computer aided design and computer aided geometric design.
